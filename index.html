About the project

QUADX is a four-legged robotic platform designed to explore intelligent locomotion, motion control, and dynamic balance.

The robot combines a 12-DOF servo-driven mechanism with inverse kinematics, inertial sensing, balance control, and gait generation to achieve controlled and coordinated leg movements.

QUADX provides a flexible platform for developing and testing advanced quadruped robotics algorithms.

Control system

Transforms high-level commands into smooth and stable motion. IMU feedback is processed by the PID controller to compensate pitch (Y) and roll (X) in real time.
